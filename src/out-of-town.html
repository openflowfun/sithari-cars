How it works

Four steps, and we do the running around in between.

Step 1

Call us about the car

We will verify every detail of the car with you over the phone and send extra photos or a video. If you want it, we can tailor finance, insurance or a warranty package at the same time.

Step 2

Get it checked

Most of our vehicles are AA appraised for your peace of mind. If you would rather have a comprehensive pre-purchase inspection, we will arrange it through AA or VTNZ on your behalf.

Step 3

Agree the deal in writing

We take the car off the market and reserve it for you on a deposit, or no deposit if you are financing. You get a Vehicle Offer and Sale Agreement with every detail listed before we ask you for anything.

Step 4

We deliver to your door

Once payment clears we arrange delivery nationwide at competitive rates through our trusted vehicle transporters, to your door or the nearest depot.
